Cosmic Cookies 🍪

the easiest chocolate-chips cookies in this galaxy.

⏰ 25 minutes  |  🍪 12 cookies  |  ⭐ Super easy

What you'll need

  • cup of flour
  • 1/2 cup of butter
  • 1/2 cup of suger
  • 1 egg
  • Lots of chocolate chips!

let's bake!

  1. Mix the butter and sugar.
  2. Add the egg and flour .
  3. Stir in chocolate chips.
  4. Bake for 12 minutes at 180°C.

Grandma's secret: Save one cookie for yourself. Or five.

Happy baking! ♥
